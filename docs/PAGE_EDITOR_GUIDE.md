# Editing ALCO website pages

Open **Admin → Website → Pages**. Each row shows the page's public address; published rows link to the visitor page. Home, About, Services, Insights, Tools, FAQ, Contact, Consultation and legal pages keep their existing addresses.

## Update an existing page

1. Open the page and edit its draft copy. The Home editor also exposes its service and discovery headings; Home and About expose their final call to action. Service cards, team profiles, packages, FAQs and slider images are managed in their own Website sections.
2. Use **Save & publish page** when visitors should see the changes. This action saves the current form and publishes it in one step.
3. Use **Save** to keep an unfinished draft. A draft does not change the visitor page. **Publish saved draft** publishes the last saved draft without saving edits still open in the form.

The page title is used in browser tabs and social previews. The search engine description is also used for page metadata. Keep both concise and accurate.

## Create a page

1. Choose **New page**, enter a title, and check the generated slug. A custom slug produces a URL such as `/pages/our-approach`. The slug is locked after creation to avoid breaking links.
2. Pick **Blank page**, **Information page**, **Services overview**, or **How we work**. A template adds editable starter blocks; switching templates replaces draft blocks, so make this choice before writing detailed copy.
3. Fill in the hero heading, introduction and search description. Add, remove or reorder page sections. Text sections support headings, links and lists.
4. Save the page, then choose **Save & publish page**. Unpublished custom pages return a 404 and stay out of the sitemap.
5. To add the published page to the header or footer, go to **Website → Navigation**, choose the menu, and select or enter its `/pages/...` address.

Preview the public page after publishing. Placeholder wording in starter blocks should be replaced before publishing.
